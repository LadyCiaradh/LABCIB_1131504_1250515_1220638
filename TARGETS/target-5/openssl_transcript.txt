Target 5 - transcript openssl (decriptacao da assinatura e lookup)

# A "assinatura" (sig.bin, do Target 4) abre com a chave PRIVADA, nao com a publica.
$ openssl pkeyutl -decrypt -inkey ..\target-4\key.der -keyform DER -in ..\target-4\sig.bin -out hash.json
$ type hash.json
{"hash": "e07fcd7e6951d04659c4a8debe8e63de"}

# Lookup do MD5 na rainbow table fornecida.
$ findstr "e07fcd7e6951d04659c4a8debe8e63de" ..\..\..\Rainbowtablee.txt > rainbow_lookup.txt
$ type rainbow_lookup.txt
cncazezuxmihjpgrddzjnemmyzajtwcyqufqqrqcrsoqhsdtnnwwa e07fcd7e6951d04659c4a8debe8e63de

# Confirmacao (round-trip): md5(password) == hash recuperado.
